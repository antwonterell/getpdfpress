How the compressor works

No magic, just three honest passes that stop as soon as your target is hit.

1. Light optimization

First we rewrite the PDF structure, strip hidden edit history and metadata, and consolidate duplicate resources. Already-lean files often finish here with zero quality change.

2. Smart recompression

If the file is still over target, page images are recompressed at a quality calculated from your exact byte budget. Text stays sharp; oversized scans slim down.

3. Verify and retry

We measure the actual result. Overshot? We retry with tighter settings, up to three passes. And if nothing beats your original, you get your original back, never a bigger file.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no daily task limit, no signup, and no watermark. Sensible caps (25MB per file) keep the service fast for everyone.

Are my files safe?

Files are processed over TLS encryption and automatically deleted from the server within 30 minutes. We never sell files, and analytics never sees file contents or names. PDF to Word and Word to PDF additionally use ConvertAPI for the conversion itself, disclosed in our privacy policy.

Why did my file miss the exact target?

Some documents cannot reach a target readably, like a 50-page scan into 200KB. We return the smallest readable result and tell you honestly, instead of shipping an unreadable file that technically hits the number.

Do you add watermarks or limit downloads?

No watermarks, no download limits, no account walls. You upload, we process, you download. That is the whole business.
