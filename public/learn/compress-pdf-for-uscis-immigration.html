What the actual USCIS file limits are

Use USCIS's official tips for filing forms online and the instructions shown for your specific form. Verify the current per-file limit, allowed formats and evidence organization before changing the document.

Evidence packets can contain many different kinds of pages. Scans, photos, fonts and export settings all affect file size. Inspect the actual PDF and receiving instructions instead of estimating a limit or size from page count.

Keep originals and verify the exact downloaded bytes before uploading. Some headroom may be useful, but neither a smaller file nor a clear preview guarantees acceptance.

Why immigration PDFs get so large

Three culprits cause almost every oversized immigration upload:

  • Color scans. Color may carry important evidence. Do not change to grayscale unless the form's instructions allow it and no required information is lost. Savings vary by image and encoding.
  • Phone-camera scans. Crop irrelevant surroundings while keeping all document edges and required content. Use even lighting and compare the result with the original.
  • Resolution. Follow any stated resolution requirement. Compare small print and marks before lowering resolution; no DPI value guarantees readability for every document.

Step-by-step: compress without losing readability

  1. Organize related pages. Follow the form's instructions for original documents, translations and supporting evidence. Merge related pages only when that organization is accepted.
  2. Check the required limit before compressing. Follow the upload instructions for your specific form. Our 500KB compressor uses a fixed, best-effort target and has no compression-level selector. A file that already meets the stated limit and is readable may not need further compression.
  3. Zoom to 150% and inspect. Check signatures, notary seals, stamps, and small print on the compressed copy. If details become unclear, return to the original file. Consider rescanning or splitting documents only when the receiving portal allows it.
  4. If the receiving service permits separate files, use the Split tool to extract pages, then arrange all required pages into clearly labelled files that follow its instructions.

How to organize multi-document evidence

Organize evidence in the categories and order requested by the form. Use clear filenames and include translations or certifications where the instructions require them. This guide does not decide what evidence your case needs.

Final checks before you upload

  • File format and exact size meet the current instructions for this form and evidence field.
  • Every page is right-side up and in order.
  • Stamps, seals, signatures, and handwriting are legible at 150% zoom.
  • Required translations and certifications are included according to the form instructions.
  • Filename follows the current portal instructions.

One honest caveat: getPDFpress targets are best-effort, and we're a PDF tool, not an immigration advisor. For case-specific questions about what evidence to file, consult an immigration attorney or USCIS directly.

Frequently asked questions

What file size does USCIS accept?

Check the current instructions for your specific USCIS form and evidence field, together with the official online-filing guidance. Do not infer a universal size limit from this guide or from a compressor's target.

Will compressing my documents cause an RFE?

This tool cannot predict whether USCIS will request more evidence. Inspect stamps, seals, signatures, and small print in the output before uploading. If details become unclear, use the original or a clearer scan that meets the instructions for your form. Keep a backup of every original document.

Should I scan in color or grayscale for USCIS?

Follow the form's instructions for color and image quality. Keep color when required or when it carries relevant information. If grayscale is allowed, compare every stamp, mark and faint detail before using the smaller copy.

My evidence is 200 pages. One file or many?

Follow the form's organization and attachment instructions. Use separate files only where permitted, retain every required page, and label the parts clearly. Ask USCIS or a qualified adviser if the permitted organization is unclear.

What if my scan will not stay readable under the limit?

Do not reduce quality until required details disappear. Split only when separate files are allowed. Otherwise improve the source or ask about an accepted alternative. A 500KB or 200KB tool target does not establish the limit for an immigration filing.
