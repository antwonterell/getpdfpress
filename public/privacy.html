Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 21, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

getPDFpress is built with privacy as a core principle. We process your PDFs entirely in your browser or on secure servers that immediately delete files after processing.

Data Collection

Files You Upload

  • Browser processing: When possible, files are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your device.
  • Server processing: For server-side operations, files are uploaded, processed, and immediately deleted after download or within 1 hour, whichever comes first.
  • No storage: We do not store, analyze, or retain your files.
  • No human access: No humans ever see your files. All processing is automated.

Analytics Data

We use privacy-friendly analytics (Google Analytics with IP anonymization) to understand:

  • Number of visitors
  • Which tools are most popular
  • General geographic location (country/city level)
  • Device type and browser

We do NOT track: Individual users, email addresses, or personal information.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics)
  • Remembering your dark mode preference (optional)

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics with IP anonymization
  • Hosting Provider: Hostinger and Render.com for infrastructure

Data Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Files are processed in isolated, temporary containers
  • Automatic file deletion after processing
  • No user accounts = no password breaches

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal information or store files, there's no personal data to access, modify, or delete. You maintain full control over your files at all times.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
